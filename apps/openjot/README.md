# OpenJot

A free, open-source money tracker for Thai bank slips, and an alternative to Meowjot. OpenJot is published by Trajectonomics and was formerly MooJot; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meowjot or any bank.

Website: https://trajectonomics.com/openjot/ (built in [`apps/site`](../site/README.md)). Download: https://trajectonomics.com/openjot/download (source in [`site/`](site/README.md)).
