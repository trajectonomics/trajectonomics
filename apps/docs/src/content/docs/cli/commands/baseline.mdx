`traj baseline` shows the Baseline a Projection starts from, and what each income and expense Account
ran at. The Baseline is derived on demand from the stored rows and refuses what it cannot state.

## What a Baseline holds

Only asset and liability leaf accounts cross, and only when their balance at the date is non-zero.
Equity, income and expense never do: equity is the contra side of an opening balance, and income and
expense are flows. A header account never appears either, while an account closed with
`traj account close` does, because closing keeps its history. Net worth is assets minus liabilities,
and it counts nothing else.

## `traj baseline rates`

Show what each income and expense Account ran at over whole recorded months.

- `--as-of <string>` — the date the rates are stated at, as `YYYY-MM-DD`; required
- `--months <number>` — whole calendar months in the window, 3 to 36; default 12

The window is the whole calendar months ending at the last full month on or before `--as-of`. Each
income and expense Account with activity gets its total over the months the record covers, and a
run-rate: the total divided by those months, or by the times its one calendar month falls in them
when the window spans a year and every active month is that month. The rate divides by the months
the record covers, never by the months you asked for, so a short record does not read as a low rate.
A closed Account, a negative total and a zero total are stated and marked as left out.

```bash exec
traj baseline rates --as-of 2026-04-30
```

```text output
rates (1 row)
Account  Type     Per    Amount  Months  In plan
Food     expense  month   30.13  1 of 4  yes

totals (2 rows)
Type      Total  Monthly
income     0.00     0.00
expense  120.50    30.13

Covers 4 of the 12 months asked: 2026-01 to 2026-04.

Amounts are rounded half away from zero to the satang.
```

The Quickstart has one expense, Food, on 2026-03-01, and the window covers 2026-01 to 2026-04, so
120.50 THB over four months is `30.13` THB a month. The read states; the caller applies.
[`traj scenario apply --template --from-history`](/cli/commands/scenario/) turns the carried rows
into a Scenario's income and spending Flows.

## `traj baseline show`

Show the Baseline a Projection starts from at a date.

- `--as-of <string>` — the date the Baseline is stated at, as `YYYY-MM-DD`; required

```bash
traj baseline show --as-of 2026-03-31 --json  # The Baseline a Projection would start from at one date.
```

On the [Quickstart](/cli/)'s funded ledger the Baseline is one row: `Cash`, an asset, `879.50`
THB. The equity account `Opening` and the expense account `Food` do not cross.

## When a Baseline refuses

A balance with no representation refuses the whole Baseline at exit code `3` rather than dropping or
clamping the account. An overdrawn asset and an overpaid loan are both negative once stated, so
carrying either would make the Baseline disagree with the ledger without saying so.

On the Quickstart's ledger without its opening balance, Cash holds `-120.50` THB and the command
fails with `NEGATIVE_AMOUNT` and `Cash holds -120.50 THB, which is negative once stated as an
asset`. The way to a Baseline that succeeds is a funded ledger: create the equity account, then post
the [opening balance](/cli/commands/tx/) that keeps every asset and liability positive.
