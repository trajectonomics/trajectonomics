# Where money goes orders the saving and the drawing, keeps reserve floors, and plans moves between accounts

The order in which a plan saves what is left over and draws savings when money is short decides as much as the amounts do, and until now it lived in `cashflow` inside the document where nobody could see it. [cli ADR 0070](../../../cli/docs/adr/0070-routing-shares-reserve-floors-and-transfers-are-the-documents-and-projection-run-states-each-step.md) lets `traj` take a share of what is left, a reserve floor, a target that keeps its value, and a `transfer` Flow, and states what each did. The page puts them in one place. Every figure is `traj`'s and every amount is the text the person typed ([ADR 0002](0002-the-cli-is-the-only-source-of-figures.md)).

## Decision

- **The page.** `/routing/`, "Where money goes", listed when the selected plan has a document, directly after Cash flow, and the Cash flow page links to it. Cash flow has no heading of its own in the navigation, so the entry sits in that heading-less group, not under Plan.
- **Four sections, each with what it did beside it.** Rows on the left, a small stacked-bar chart of the plan's years on the right (stacked on a phone). The chart reads the Snapshot's own `projection run --breakdown`, or the What-if's run while the session holds a change: what each surplus step filled (`surplus` lines), what each `withdraw` entry drew (`drawn` lines, told apart by `entry`), what each saving into a `contribute` Account took (`contribution` lines) and what each transfer moved (the `transfers` table, with a sentence for each year part of a plan did not move). `toChartNumber` places a mark; no figure is added.
  1. **When there is money left over**: the `surplus` steps. A row reads "Fill Emergency fund up to 300,000.00 THB in today's money", "Put 30% of what is left into Funds" or "Everything else into Savings". Add, edit, remove and reorder.
  2. **Which savings come first**: the `contribute` priority. Reorder only: its members are every asset a Contribution targets (`CONTRIBUTION_UNROUTED`), which the Income and Assets pages own.
  3. **When money is short**: the `withdraw` entries. "Take from Funds", "Take from Savings, down to 100,000.00 THB", "Then the rest of Savings".
  4. **Planned moves between your accounts**: the `transfer` Flows.
- **The routing echo.** A row whose target keeps its value also says what `projection run`'s `routing` echo says of it: "At the start 300,000.00, grown by inflation: 346,000.00 at the horizon." The amount at the horizon is the engine's `resolved.horizon` ([#470](https://github.com/trajectonomics/trajectonomics/pull/470)); a run that states none says only "at the start, grown by inflation". The page computes nothing.
- **Reordering.** A row is dragged, or moved by its Up and Down buttons (the way a keyboard and a phone reorder). A drop saves at once. The buttons change the order on the page only and show "Done", "Try this order" and "Cancel", because a keyboard user has no drop to commit. An order that breaks the last-step rule is not sent: the row says so.
- **The last surplus step takes everything.** The view model refuses a list whose last step has a share or a cap (`lastStepProblem`) and says so on that row; a step that takes less is added before the last one, and a step that takes everything at the end. The CLI's own refusal (`SURPLUS_SHARE_LAST`) lands on its row too, from its positional path (`document.cashflow.surplus.1.share`, or `details.documentPath` in a what-if).
- **A floor adds its "rest of" entry.** A floor on an Account that has no later plain entry adds one at the end of the list ("Then the rest of Savings"), so the money under the floor is still there once the other entries are used. A floored entry that nothing later takes the rest of says so on its row. Which floors are allowed after which is the engine's (`WITHDRAW_FLOOR_ORDER`), and its refusal lands on the row.
- **An illiquid asset is never offered** to a step, a floor or a transfer's source, and the drawers and the section say why: the plan cannot take money from it. A transfer may land in one, or pay a loan down. A plan that already routes one by hand keeps it in the select.
- **A transfer.** From, to, how much (all of it, an amount, or a share of what the source holds), how often, when (a date, a Milestone, or a date the household names, written as a plain date as a payout's is, [ADR 0017](0017-an-employers-share-and-an-accounts-payout-and-fee-are-set-in-the-forms-that-already-edit-them.md)) and "count any gain for tax". All of it is a `derive` that reads the source; a share is a `scale` of it over 10,000 rounded down, as the engine's own share step is, with the percent moved to basis points by text. An amount may keep its value in today's money (`growth: "inflation"`). `realise` is off by default, written only when on, and explained: a gain on listed shares and on mutual funds held by an individual is generally not taxed in Thailand, and a fund-to-fund move is the usual case. A `derive` of another shape is another item and is shown as computed.
- **Saving is one `apply-plan` Edit** with the hash the page read ([ADR 0008](0008-the-dashboard-edits-scenarios-through-traj-on-loopback.md)): a change is data (`RoutingChange`: a whole routing list, a transfer put in, a transfer removed) that is turned into the document (`documentWith`) and into a What-if session (`sessionWith`), so the two cannot disagree. A refusal's path names a row by position (`cashflow.withdraw.1.downTo`) or a transfer by its position or id (`flows.3.target`); the page maps it to its row and field (`refusal.ts`, `shown.ts`) and shows the CLI's words once, in the open drawer or on the row.
- **A what-if.** Every change can be tried ([ADR 0010](0010-the-what-if-session-reads-through-traj-on-loopback.md)): a routing list is one Set of the whole list (`cashflow.surplus`, which `--set` replaces), a transfer is tried as a Flow is (`withTriedFlow`), a removal as a Removal. A drawer that tries a value starts from the plan as the session shows it (`shownPlan`: the Sets put in, the removed Flows out and the added Flows in), so a new try keeps the earlier ones on the same document, as #446 and #452 did for Accounts. A drawer that saves starts from the stored plan, so a save never carries a try into the plan. The bar words a routing Set without its rows, which hold the person's own targets and shares.
- **The rows show the session's lists.** Each routing list is drawn from the plan as the session shows it, and a reorder or a removal that is tried is built from that list, so "Try this order" after a floor try keeps the floor. While the session changes a list, saving a change to it (a drop, Done, Edit, Remove) waits with "Apply or revert the what-if first", because a save from the session's list would put the try in the plan; trying still works, and "Add" opens as a try.
- **A row keeps its key as it moves.** The rows of a list being reordered are keyed by their first position, so a second press of Up or Down moves the same row, and focus goes to the row's other button when the one pressed has reached the edge.
- **A growth is one of three.** An amount stays as typed, keeps its value in today's money, or grows by a fixed percent a year (`{ bps }`, typed as a percent and written as basis points by text). A stored `{ bps }` is read as fixed, so editing a row never turns it into another growth.
- **A what-if's refusal lands on its row.** While the session holds a `cashflow.*` Set, the refusal of its projection read is placed from `details.documentPath` (`withdraw.1.downTo`), as an Edit's is.
- **Two readers of `withdraw`** (`asset-owned.ts`, the Withdrawals page's default portfolio) read each entry's Account (`withdrawAccounts`) instead of the entry, so a floored entry is not dropped.
- **Controls** appear only when the server answers the token and never in the Demo snapshot. The Demo snapshot and its generator are untouched.

## Considered Options

- **One Set for each field of a step.** Rejected: a step is addressed by position, a reorder would change what every path names, and the list has no id. The whole list is one Set.
- **Letting a list end in a share and leaving the refusal to the CLI.** Rejected for the page: the rule is cheap to state and the person should read it before the save, not after. The CLI's refusal still lands on its row.
- **Computing a grown target on the page.** Rejected: it would reimplement the engine's whole-year growth (cli ADR 0070); it reads the engine's `resolved` instead.
- **Offering a household anchor as a person's age in the drawer.** Not done: the anchor lives in the household document and a second write would break "one Edit"; #447's follow-up and the payout form do the same.

## Consequences

- `scripts/routing.test.ts` runs the issue's story against the workspace binary: a target in today's money, a 30 per cent share, a floor and a PVD to RMF move, a reorder that changes the figures, a floor tried with the earlier try kept, and refusals at their rows.
- A change in the CLI's routing output fails `src/lib/contract/` as before. `echo.ts` reads the echo's `resolved` and is tested against the CLI's own golden (`run-routing.output`).
