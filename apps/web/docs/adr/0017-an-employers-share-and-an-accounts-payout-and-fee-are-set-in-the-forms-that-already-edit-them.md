# An employer's share, and an account's payout and fee, are set in the forms that already edit them

A provident-fund match, dividends paid out and a fund's fee are things people know about their own accounts. `traj` carries them since [cli ADR 0067](../../../cli/docs/adr/0067-an-accounts-payout-and-fee-and-an-employers-share-are-the-documents-and-projection-run-states-what-each-did.md) (engine: sim ADR 0026, 0027, 0028). The Dashboard sets them where it already edits a salary and an account, and reads what each did from `projection run`. Every figure is the CLI's text ([ADR 0002](0002-the-cli-is-the-only-source-of-figures.md)); no share, rounding or date is a default anywhere.

## Decision

- **Your employer also pays into.** The salary form (earned income) gains an account picker (asset Accounts that are not `liquid: false`), a share of this salary typed in per cent, and a rounding with none chosen. It writes one Contribution Flow, `<salary>-employer`, with `funding: "employer"`, no label, and a `derive` of `pay` (the `flow` Measure of the salary) and `share` (`scale` of `pay`, `num` the basis points of the typed percent moved by text, `den` 10000). It takes the salary's frequency, start, end and Milestone gates, so it lands when the salary lands and stops when it stops. The salary and its Flow are one `apply-plan` Edit and are kept in step: each save of the salary rebuilds the Flow's dates from the salary, clearing the section removes the Flow, and deleting the salary removes both. The ids are a convention only the Dashboard reads, as in [ADR 0011](0011-a-real-asset-is-one-form-and-one-card-over-parts-the-cli-already-has.md): a Flow that follows the id but not the shape is another item, which the form never edits and refuses to overwrite. The rounding is required because `scale` needs one (the issue did not list it); like the share it starts empty. A salary that is not earned cannot carry a share (the engine stops it with earned income), so unticking earned under a share is a problem at that field.
- **The salary row** reads "Employer adds 5% to <account>", the percent moved by text from the basis points (`percentWords`). The employer Flow is a contribution, so it is no card of its own.
- **Pays out and Yearly fee.** The asset account form gains two optional sections, neither offered for an illiquid asset or a debt. An illiquid asset keeps a `fee` held by hand untouched; a debt drops both. A section left empty is none, and emptying one that was saved removes it. Pays out: a yearly per cent, how often (monthly, quarterly, twice a year, yearly), from when (now, a Milestone, a date the household names, a date) and a required rounding. The household's date is an anchored Flow date the document already holds, written as a plain date: a payout's own date is not pinned by the household. The kind of income for tax (dividends, interest, other investment income) is asked only when the plan has a Levy, and picks the label `income.section40_4.dividends`, `.interest` or `income.section40_4`; with none chosen no label is written, and a label the form does not write is kept. The payout's end, end Milestone and prepayments are kept as they are. Yearly fee: a per cent and a rounding. Percents are sent as the text typed (`"3.5"`); `traj` stores and states them as `"3.50"`.
- **The account card** reads "Pays out 3.5% a year from retire" (the Milestone's id; a date reads as the date) and "Fee 1.5% a year", with how it is paid beneath. Under it a "Year by year" list states, per roll-up, the payout (the `payout` lines of `--breakdown`) and the fee (the `fees` table) exactly as `traj` states them.
- **Fees and payouts over the plan** (amended: the engine states them, sim ADR 0027, 0028 and [cli ADR 0067](../../../cli/docs/adr/0067-an-accounts-payout-and-fee-and-an-employers-share-are-the-documents-and-projection-run-states-what-each-did.md)). `projection run` carries `accountLifetimes` (one `{ account, lifetimeFees?, lifetimePayouts? }` per Account that declares a fee or a payout, in Scenario order) and a plan-wide `lifetimeFees`; the contract decodes both as optional, on `projection run` and, since it extends it, on `projection path`. Nothing is summed in the page ([ADR 0002](0002-the-cli-is-the-only-source-of-figures.md)). The account card shows "Fees over the plan" and "Paid out over the plan", each the CLI's text for that Account (`accountLifetimesOf`), above the year-by-year list; a line shows only when the CLI states it, or, in a What-if session, when the baseline's run stated it and the tried run does not: it then says None with the baseline's figure struck through (#455). Home's Net worth card shows "Fees over the plan: ฿X" from the primary Scenario's run when `lifetimeFees` is above zero. The page compares to zero only through the contract's own form: money is a decimal string (`-?\d+(\.\d+)?`), so a figure is zero when its digits are all zero (`isZeroAmount`) and below zero when it starts with `-` (`isAboveZero`); it reads text and never converts it to a number. A run that states none, or zero, shows no line. While a What-if session is open the card reads the what-if's own run, so the tried figure is shown, with the baseline's figure struck through beside it when it differs (as the card's other changed fields), and the Overview's what-if lists "Fees over the plan" as the tried figure with the baseline's under it and no change figure, because `projection compare` states none. The Demo is not grown: it declares no fee or payout, so its run states none.
- **The Cash flow page.** Payouts are lines under money in beside the declared income ("Paid out by <account>"), because the CLI's money in includes them, and a source band of the diagram. The employer's money is a section of its own, "from your employer", shown only in a year it pays, with the CLI's amount per line, no total, and a note that it never reaches cash. In the diagram it is one band in from the employer and one band on into the account, so the two columns stay as even as the CLI states them; the two bands are one line, and picking either picks its table row.
- **Refusals land at their fields.** `traj` names a Flow by position (`document.flows.1.label`), so the form turns the position into the Flow's id first (`namedRefusal`), over the ids of the document the save actually sent. A refusal under an Account's `payout` or `fee` lands at the field of the key straight after the section (a deeper one, such as `payout.prepayments.0.bps`, is no field and shows as the general problem); one on the employer's Flow lands in its section, including `EMPLOYER_MEMBER_LABEL`.
- **What-if.** Each field can be tried. A payout or fee the Account did not have, or one taken away, is one Set of the whole Account (`accounts.<id>`), because a Set replaces an existing path and cannot add or delete one (the rule [ADR 0013](0013-the-withdrawals-page-reads-what-a-spending-rule-pays-and-writes-it-as-one-flow.md) states for a Flow; `withTriedItem` serves both lists). A changed fee is a Set of the field. Deleting a salary says its employer Flow goes too. The employer's Flow is tried beside the salary: new is an Addition, changed a Set, cleared switched off. A drawer that tries a value starts from the Account (and the employer share) as the session shows it, so a second try carries the first, because a whole-item try replaces every earlier change to that item. The overlay now also takes a whole-item Set, so the cards show it, and the session bar words a tried account without its shares. The account card reads the what-if's own run for its years and totals while a session is open.
- **Household-anchored dates follow the salary.** `scenario household` moves `<salary>-employer` with the salary when an anchor sets the salary's `start` or `end`: the CLI writes the same date into every employer Contribution whose `derive` reads that salary's gross ([cli ADR 0048](../../../cli/docs/adr/0048-a-household-is-a-sibling-of-the-scenario-with-anchors-written-on-save.md), the employer rule, #447). The Dashboard does nothing for it.
- **The Demo** is left alone: its snapshot is at about 99% of Biome's formatter limit ([#378](https://github.com/trajectonomics/trajectonomics/issues/378)), and nothing in it needs these fields.

## Considered Options

- **A lifetime total summed by the page.** Rejected: arithmetic on money ([ADR 0002](0002-the-cli-is-the-only-source-of-figures.md)). The engine now states the total, so the page reads it.
- **Pre-filling the rounding or the share, or a usual kind of income.** Rejected: a default reads as the product's advice, and `traj` ships none.
- **Putting the employer's money through the cash node once.** Rejected: it never enters cash (sim ADR 0026), and one band would break the even columns the identity gives.

## Consequences

- The Income and Assets pages say nothing new about a plan that has none of these fields: every output is as it was.
- A `traj` older than #437 refuses `funding: "employer"` and `payout`/`fee`; the CLI the Dashboard ships with has them.
- Tests: view models (`employer*.test.ts`, `account-income.test.ts`, `account-card.test.ts`), the forms and cards at phone width (`account-income.svelte.test.ts`), and `scripts/income.test.ts` and `income-try.test.ts` against the workspace `traj`: a 5% share is 30,000.00 over six months of salary, a quarterly payout of 3.5% and a 1% fee on 736,600.00 are 12,732.88 and 7,316.29 in 2027 (worked by hand in `income.test-support.ts`), and half a per cent in a What-if session charges what the same walk gives.
