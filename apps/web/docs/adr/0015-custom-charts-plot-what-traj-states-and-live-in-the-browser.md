# Custom charts plot what `traj` states, and live in the browser

People ask their own questions of the Snapshot: "income against spending and tax", "each Account's value". The Projection page lets a person pick some of the yearly figures `traj` already states and draw them together. A chart is a view, not a plan: it is kept in the browser, in no Scenario and not in the ledger, for the reason [ADR 0012](0012-the-tax-allowances-page-reads-what-traj-says-each-allowance-could-take.md) keeps view settings out of the Scenario. Every figure on screen is the CLI's string ([ADR 0002](0002-the-cli-is-the-only-source-of-figures.md)).

## Decision

- **Only stated series.** The catalogue (`src/lib/view/charts/`) is built from the projection runs the Snapshot already holds, with no new read, so `leaves.test.ts` and the Demo snapshot are unchanged. A series is one of: net worth at the close of the year (with the engine's real figure), each Account's value (`--accounts`, nominal and real, read on the roll-up ends), income, expenses, contributions, unfunded contributions, interest and withdrawals (the annual table), each cash-flow line by Flow and by category (`--breakdown`), each Levy's tax due and taxable income (`taxYears`), each spending rule's paid amount (nominal and real) and what heirs would receive (nominal and real). Marginal and effective rates (#404) and the account income, costs, routing and transfer totals (#409, #416) are not in the contract yet, so they are not offered and there is no second axis; each is a new id when its table lands. A chart of a what-if lens is not offered: the page draws the Snapshot's run.
- **No money arithmetic.** A point is the CLI's string; `toChartNumber` places its mark and the tooltip, the legend and the table show the string. A stacked chart stacks the placed numbers for drawing only (`stackSpans`): positives upward, negatives downward from zero. Nothing is summed, differenced or divided as a figure, and no total of a stack is shown.
- **Terms.** Net worth, Account values, a spending rule's paid amount and what heirs receive have a real figure from `traj`, so they follow the page's terms control (nominal, real, both). Every other series is drawn nominal and named "(nominal)" when the terms ask for real. A stacked chart draws one set of terms: it draws real only when every series it draws has a real figure from `traj`, and otherwise (and for "both") it draws nominal and says so. Real figures are never stacked on nominal ones.
- **Where a point sits.** A point sits at the day its period ends, placed by the day count (`dayNumber`, whole-number arithmetic on the date's text) and labelled by that date ("30 Oct 2027"). It carries the period `traj` states: the annual rows' `from` and `to` and their `partial` flag, a tax year's `from`, `to` and `partial`, and for a figure stated on a date (an Account's value, a spending rule's year, the estate) the annual row that ends on that date. The tooltip and the table name the period as the annual table does ("31 Oct 2026 – 30 Oct 2027"), and a part year as such ("31 Oct – 31 Dec 2053, part year"). A part-year bar is drawn faded and the chart says so. A column holds the periods that end on one day: series whose periods end on different days (the Demo's projection years end on 30 Oct, its tax years on 31 Dec) sit in their own columns, never stacked into one, and a note names which end when. No period is dropped, moved or merged. A point with no period of its own (a figure `traj` states for a date that is no annual row's end) is named by that date.
- **Storage.** `localStorage`, key `traj.charts`, one JSON document `{ "format": "trajectonomics-charts", "version": 1, "charts": [...] }`, each chart `{ id, name, kind, series }` with `kind` one of `line`, `bars`, `stacked-line`, `stacked-bars` and `series` the ids above (at most 8; at most 50 charts). It is keyed by series ids and not by plan, so a chart works on every plan. The document holds no figure. Every read and write is in `src/client/` (`charts.svelte.ts`) in a try/catch; with storage unavailable (a private window) the page still creates, shows, edits and deletes charts for the visit and says they are not kept, and that export keeps them. Text in storage that cannot be read (malformed, or a newer version) is no charts, and the page says why. Before the next save replaces it, the unread text is kept under `traj.charts.unread` and the page says so. Only the `storage` event for the `traj.charts` key re-reads, so two tabs agree; the address and the colour scheme do not. A read the browser refuses never replaces the charts held in memory, so with storage blocked the charts of this visit stay.
- **Export and import.** The same document is the exported file (`trajectonomics-charts.json`). An import is read strictly: anything that is not that document (not JSON, another format, a newer version (its version is named before its shape is read, since a newer shape may differ), a kind or series list that does not fit) is refused whole with a sentence and changes nothing. A good file is added after the charts already held, each under an id not yet used; it is refused when the total would pass 50.
- **A Snapshot whose series no longer exist.** A chart that names a series the selected plan lacks (no tax, an Account that was removed, a rule that was deleted) draws what it can and lists each missing series by name with its reason ("this plan has no tax", or "this plan has no tax for th-cgt" when the plan has another Levy) above the chart; a chart with none of its series says so and draws nothing. It does not fail. In the drawer a chosen series that no plan lists is shown under "Not in this Snapshot" so it can be taken off. A series no plan of the Snapshot knows (a chart from another ledger, or an imported file) has no name to show, so it is shown by its id. Account series ids are ledger ids, so an exported chart of Account values matches Accounts on the same ledger only.
- **The page.** The Projection chart's title is a native select (the net worth chart, "My charts", "New chart…"); the choice is the address bar's `chart`. "New chart" and Edit open the right-side drawer (name, kind, series by group; Create is enabled once one series is chosen, and an empty name takes the first series' name). Edit, Duplicate and Delete (after a question) sit beside the chart. A series the selected plan lacks is greyed out in the picker with its reason, and a group the plan has none of says so once. Each chart has a table of every plotted value as the text alternative (`details`, scrolling inside its own box, so the page does not scroll sideways), and the chart is a keyboard-operable slider as the other charts are. The title's select is as wide as its longest chart name up to the width of the page, and shrinks inside it. Focus returns to the picker when the drawer closes or a chart is deleted. Hide amounts masks the table, the tooltip and the axis.
- **No chart library.** The charts use the existing line layout and a new `columnLayout` (bars, stacked bars, stacked line) in `src/lib/chart/`, drawn by the same SVG component with a four-token palette whose second round is told apart by dash or fade (a custom line chart takes the wide palette, a second round is dotted, and a real line is dashed, so no two series of up to eight lines look alike) ([ADR 0003](0003-astro-with-react-islands-and-no-chart-library.md), kept by [ADR 0006](0006-svelte-5-on-sveltekit-replaces-astro-and-react.md)).

## Considered Options

- **A file next to the ledger.** Rejected: a second store for `traj` to own and back up, for a view.
- **Keying a chart by plan.** Rejected: a person's question ("income against tax") is the same on every plan.
- **A sum or ratio series ("savings rate").** Rejected: it is arithmetic on money (ADR 0002); `traj` would have to state it.

## Consequences

- A change in the shape of the stored document is a new `version`; an older page refuses a newer file instead of misreading it.
- `src/lib/view/charts/charts.test.ts` pins the catalogue, the greyed series and reasons, the round trip and the bad imports; `src/client/charts.svelte.test.ts` pins storage available and unavailable; `src/components/charts/charts.svelte.test.ts` renders a stacked-bars chart of income, expenses and tax on two plans and with amounts hidden.
