# `traj projection allowances` states what more each tax allowance could take and what it would save

Status: accepted. Ships in 0.8.0.

A person who files in Thailand can lower a year's tax by putting more into RMF, ThaiESG and the other retirement allowances, up to caps that depend on income and on each other. A person, an agent and the Dashboard need one read that says, for every whole tax year of a taxed plan, how much more each allowance could take and the tax it would save. The engine answers it for one Settlement period (`contributionSavings`, sim ADR-0022). This leaf runs the plan, hands the engine each period and states the answer in the CLI's money, with the year's assumptions (ADR-0042). It computes no figure of its own (ADR-0036) and writes nothing.

## Decision

- **The leaf.** `traj projection allowances` takes the plan inputs of `projection run` (`--scenario`, `--as-of`, `--horizon`, `--carry-in`, `--set`, `--without`, `--decimal`; ADR-0030) and four of its own:
  - `--labels a,b` (required): the contribution Tally labels to fill, in the person's order. The order decides which fund holds the money, not how much tax falls (sim ADR-0022).
  - `--step <amount>` (required, no default): one step in major units, converted exactly by the CLI's own parser (ADR-0047). The page pre-fills 1,000; the CLI ships none, because every amount is the caller's.
  - `--budget <amount>` (optional): the most the extra contributions may add up to in each tax year.
  - `--min-saving <percent>` (optional): the least share of a step a step must save, from 0 to 100 with at most two decimals. It is turned into basis points by string handling (`12.5` is `1250`); no float is involved.
- **A label the Levy does not read is refused at `--labels`**, and the refusal lists the labels the Levies do read, taken from their `funded` tally Measures (ADR-0036 reads the contribution classes the same way). `traj schema` names the labels of the Levy `scenario tax` writes in the flag's description, from the same graph. A plan with no Levy is refused at `--scenario` with a hint that names `traj scenario tax` (ADR-0040).
- **The grain is one major unit, and a step must be larger.** The engine needs `grain`, one major unit in minor units, to tell a step that ends on a cap from one that goes past it. It refuses `grain > step`, and a `grain` equal to `step` silently turns the over-the-cap rule off. The CLI passes one major unit and refuses a `--step` that is not larger than one, in its own words (`--step: 1 is not larger than one major unit (1.00 THB)`), so neither can happen.
- **`maxSteps` is 2000 over every label of one tax year.** The engine counts a step before it knows the step will be dropped for going past a cap, so `TOO_MANY_STEPS` can fire when the answer needs exactly `maxSteps` steps. A tax year whose answer needs fewer than 2000 steps never refuses. The refusal says the year needs 2000 steps or more and tells the caller to raise `--step`, since a larger step reads the same caps in fewer steps. 2000 steps is about 0.3 s of Levy evaluation for a year, and a 1,000 THB step on the Thai caps needs about 500.
- **The result.**
  - `taxYears`: one row per Settlement period the Projection holds whole and the As-of has not passed (the year the As-of falls in is included, and needs a Carry-in record when the As-of is mid-year), with the Levy's id, `from`, `to`, `settlementDate`, `dueBefore`, `dueAfter`, `saving` and the period's `assumptions`. `saving` is the engine's own label savings added; the engine states that `dueBefore − dueAfter` equals their sum exactly (sim ADR-0022) and a test pins it, so the CLI subtracts nothing. A partial period is left out, and so is a period that ended before the As-of (a Carry-in scope record can make one appear), each with a note.
  - `allowances`: one row per period and label, with `levy`, `from`, `to`, `label`, `planned`, `extra`, `saving`, `stoppedBy` and, when `extra` is above zero, the `flow` that applies it and, when no contribution of the plan carries the label, a `hint`.
  - The echo block, the labels, the step, the budget and `minSavingBps` as understood, and `notes`, one of which says the figures are one plan's own tax rules and not advice.
  - Money is a decimal string at the Currency's scale. The human form prints one block per tax year, with what it owes now and with every suggestion, a table of the labels, and the assumptions block (ADR-0042).
- **`stoppedBy` is the engine's closed set, and the human words say it once for two causes.** `noSaving` is both "the allowance's cap is reached" and "one more step saves nothing", because the engine reports them the same way: the human form says "no more is saved (the cap is reached or the tax stops falling)". `belowMinSaving` and `budget` have a sentence each.
- **A suggestion is a Flow the caller applies, never one this leaf writes.** The `flow` is a contribution Flow in the Scenario's decimal form (ADR-0047), so `scenario apply --decimal` takes it as it is:
  - `kind: "contribution"`, `funding: "obligation"` (a lump sum paid in full, from savings when the day's cash is short; sim ADR-0021), `frequency: "once"`, `label`, `amount`, and `growth: { "bps": 0 }`, which the document requires beside an `amount`.
  - `start`: the 15th of the tax year's last month, so a Calendar day rule cannot move it into the next tax year. When the As-of is later than that 15th, `start` is the As-of, so the Flow is not dated before the Projection begins. A Flow dated on a Calendar holiday of the As-of can still shift, which a person who applies it by hand sees.
  - `target`: the Account the plan's first contribution with that label pays into, in Scenario order, skipping an Account in `cashflow.withdraw`, because the engine refuses a lump sum into an Account the plan draws from (`OBLIGATION_TARGET_WITHDRAWN`, sim ADR-0021). Without one, `target` is absent and the row's `hint` says why: no contribution carries the label (pick an asset Account, adding one if none fits), or the Account it pays into is drawn from (take it out of `cashflow.withdraw`, or add another asset Account for the purchase).
  - `id`: `<target>-<label suffix>-<year>`, where the suffix is the label's last dot-separated part with `_` as `-` (`allowance.thai_esg` is `thai-esg`) and the year is the tax year's last year, for example `rmf-fund-rmf-2026`. Without a target the id has no first part. When that id is already used by a Flow of the plan or by an earlier row of the same result, `-2`, `-3`, ... is appended, taking the first free one, so a suggestion made again after a partial apply (`rmf-fund-rmf-2026-2`) or by two Levies that read the same label never repeats an id, which `scenario apply` would refuse as `DUPLICATE_ID`. The Dashboard reads the id as a convention, as it reads ADR-0060's; the core never sees it.
- **The refusals.** `UNKNOWN_FUNDED_LABEL`, `TOO_MANY_STEPS` and `INVALID_OPTION` move from `never` to `input` in the engine refusal table, because a person can reach them through this leaf, and each has a sentence and a hint at its flag. `PERIOD_DUE_MISMATCH` stays unreachable on a correct call, since the period comes from the Projection of the same Levy, and is an `internal` error if it ever fires. An overflow or an unsafe tally is the Projection's own refusal.

## Considered options

Summing or subtracting in the CLI to state `saving` was weighed. Adding the engine's label savings is the least the year row needs, the identity with `dueBefore − dueAfter` is the engine's and is tested, and the leaf never computes a tax, a cap or a rate; asking the engine for a total would need a change in `packages/core/sim`, which this change does not touch. A default `--step` was rejected: a step is a choice about how finely to fill and the caller owns every amount. Scanning only the first Levy was rejected, because a Scenario can hold several. A `tables` array like `projection chance` was rejected for two root arrays like `taxYears` in `projection run`, so the assumptions block and a consumer that already reads `taxYears` find them where they expect. Writing the suggestions into the Scenario was rejected (ADR-0024: a projection never writes).

## Consequences

- A suggestion assumes the plan can fund it on its date. A plan with no drawable cash that day shows a run-out in `projection run` once the Flow is applied; this leaf does not run the plan again with the Flow in it.
- The result states one plan's Levy, not advice: it does not check any fund's holding rules, and the order of `--labels` decides which fund holds the money, not how much tax falls.
- A cap that is not a multiple of the step leaves up to one step of room unused; a smaller step fills more of it, at the cost of more steps.
- The web contract decodes both tables in the same change (`apps/web/src/lib/contract/projection-allowances.ts`). No page changes.
- The Thai SSF deduction is the Levy's to decide. This leaf has no rule for any label, so a change in what the Levy deducts moves the figures without a change here.
